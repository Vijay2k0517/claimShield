# 🚗 ClaimShield AI — Plain English Guide
### *A Simple, Non-Technical Overview of How ClaimShield Protects Car Insurance*

---

## 💡 What is ClaimShield AI?

Imagine you have a super-smart digital investigator that can look at a photo of a damaged car and tell you **within 2 seconds**:
1. **Is the accident genuine or staged?**
2. **Where exactly is the impact on the vehicle?**
3. **Has someone used this exact same broken car photo in another insurance claim before?**

That is **ClaimShield AI**. It helps insurance companies approve honest claims in minutes while catching fraudsters who try to submit fake or recycled accident photos.

## 🛠️ How It Works in 4 Easy Steps

### Step 1: Fast & Easy Intake 📝
* The customer or agent opens the website and enters basic car details (Make, Model, License Plate) and **uploads a photo of the damaged car**.
* Just like uploading a picture to Instagram or WhatsApp!

---

### Step 2: The AI "Scans" the Vehicle 🔍
* Our trained Computer Vision AI looks at the metal bends, broken headlights, and scratch marks.
* It compares what it sees against thousands of real crash patterns.
* It gives an instant **Fraud Risk Score** from **0% to 100%**.

---

### Step 3: The "Thermal Heatmap" (Visual Proof) 🔥
* Humans don't have to blindly trust the AI!
* The AI generates a **glowing color overlay** directly over the car photo:
  * 🔴 **Bright Red / Orange:** This is the exact focal point where the car was smashed (bonnet, headlight, bumper).
  * 🟢 **Green / Blue:** Minor secondary stress.
  * ⚪ **Clear / Untouched:** Undamaged doors, windows, and road remain crystal clear so nothing is obscured.
* Adjusters can drag a **slider** back and forth to see the photo with and without the AI heatmap.

---

### Step 4: Duplicate Check (The "Fingerprint" Match) 📑
* The AI checks the image against past settled claims in the database.
* If a fraudster downloaded a photo from the internet or tried claiming money for the same dent last year under a different name, the system **instantly alerts the team with a similarity match**!

## 🚦 Understanding the Traffic-Light Risk System

When an investigator opens the dashboard, claims are neatly sorted into 3 simple buckets:

| Risk Level | Score Range | What it Means | What the Adjuster Does |
|---|---|---|---|
| 🟢 **LOW RISK** | 0% – 39% | Clean, genuine accident matching all reported details. | **Fast-Track Approved** (Paid out immediately). |
| 🟡 **MEDIUM RISK** | 40% – 74% | Minor mismatches (e.g. repair quote higher than expected). | **Request Extra Photos or Garage Bills**. |
| 🔴 **HIGH RISK** | 75% – 100% | High probability of staged crash, tool marks, or recycled photo. | **Escalate to Special Investigation Unit (SIU)**. |
